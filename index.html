Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the studio's development philosophy, active projects, and refined roadmap.

What is Narrivex's core development philosophy? ＋

Narrivex builds minimalist, high-execution, single-purpose AI products designed for direct, professional workflows. We believe in crafting clear interfaces, maximizing speed, and avoiding excessive platform bloat or speculative architectures.

Why did the studio pivot and narrow its product focus? ＋

To deliver exceptional craftsmanship and reliable updates, we streamlined our portfolio. Transitioning from a broader initiative list allows us to maintain a sustainable workload and pour our energy entirely into perfecting our primary tools.

What is the current status of SpecuLab? ＋

The SpecuLab MVP is live and actively used for intelligence analysis, pattern discovery, and evidence synthesis. Its development remains our highest-priority production initiative, with updates continuously committed to our public GitHub repository.

What is SpecQc and when will it launch? ＋

SpecQc is our second active project, currently in development. It is an AI-assisted analysis tool built specifically to parse, check, and align technical specifications and engineering standards. We are iteratively testing prototypes with early users before a public release.

What happened to AuthorBeacon and Emma? ＋

AuthorBeacon is back in active development as our third core project, alongside SpecuLab and SpecQc. Emma was retired and is not being continued — it served as valuable exploratory research, but its direction did not fit where the studio is headed.
