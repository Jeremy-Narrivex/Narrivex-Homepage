SpecuLab

An investigation platform built to connect scattered case files, expose hidden patterns, and keep every finding tied directly to its evidence.

Visit SpecuLab Contact Narrivex

The reality of modern investigations

Real investigative work is messy. A single case often spans hundreds of PDFs, handwritten notes, interview transcripts, and spreadsheets. When your evidence lives in ten different places, critical connections get missed and simple cross-referencing eats up hours of your day.

  • Fragmented evidence: Important dates, names, and relationships end up buried across separate folders and document viewers.
  • Lost context: Finding a key detail is only half the battle—proving where it came from and verifying its accuracy takes just as long.
  • Manual fatigue: Investigators spend more time digging through documents to build timelines than actually analyzing the facts.

How SpecuLab works

  • Centralized case workspace: Bring your transcripts, filings, and evidence files together so you can review everything in context.
  • Source-bound citations: Every claim, connection, and timeline entry points back to its exact document source so your work remains transparent and audit-ready.
  • Pattern discovery: Surface non-obvious entity relationships, recurring themes, and timeline overlaps across large sets of material.
  • Clear reporting: Turn complex evidence into structured case summaries and briefings that stakeholders can easily review and verify.
