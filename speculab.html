SpecuLab

An AI-assisted investigation platform for case analysis, evidence synthesis, and pattern discovery.

Visit SpecuLab Contact Narrivex

The problem

Investigations generate far more material than any one person can hold in their head: notes, documents, timelines, witness statements, and data from many sources. Connections get missed, findings are hard to trace back to evidence, and analysts spend hours on manual cross-referencing instead of on judgment.

How SpecuLab helps

  • Case analysis — organize a case and keep every claim tied to its source.
  • Evidence synthesis — combine scattered evidence into clear, reviewable summaries.
  • Pattern discovery — surface links and recurring themes across a body of material.
