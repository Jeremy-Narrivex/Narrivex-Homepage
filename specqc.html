The challenge

Reviewing long, technical specification books and keeping up with shifting DOT standards is a time-consuming manual process. A single missed detail in a mix requirement can lead to delayed submittals, costly project holds, or compliance risks on the job site.

How SpecQC helps

SpecQC brings clarity to concrete specifications. Built for concrete producers and QC managers, SpecQC analyzes project requirements, compares them against your active mix designs and raw material data, and flags potential conflicts before they turn into problems. By automating the heavy lifting of specification review, SpecQC helps your team respond to submittal requests faster, maintain strict quality control, and support batch compliance.
