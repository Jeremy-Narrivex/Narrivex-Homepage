SpecQc

An AI-assisted engineering tool for specifications and standards analysis.

Request roadmap updates

The problem

Engineering specifications and standards are long, dense, and constantly revised. Checking that requirements are consistent, complete, and aligned with the applicable standards is slow manual work, and a missed conflict can surface late as rework, delays, or compliance risk.

How SpecQc helps

  • Specifications — parse requirements into structured, checkable items.
  • Standards review — compare documents against standards and flag gaps.
  • Engineering analysis — highlight conflicts and ambiguities for engineers to resolve.
